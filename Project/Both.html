Kamu and Muru

Whereas Muru was raised by my elder dog, Kamu has been raised by her. They both admittedly have some peculiarities that might be due to this. For example, Muru greets me at the door and Kamu knows how to stalk her prey.

They are both very interested in each other, although Kamu more than Muru. More often than not, Kamu wants to play while Muru wants to relax. They aren't good at sharing - if there is one bed, Muru sleeps in it. If there is one toy, Kamu plays with it - or eats it, as was the case with one unfortunate trip to the vet.

Both are also very interested in me, which is understandable - I provide the food. I am prime time entertainment. Kamu follows me everywhere I let her and tries to sit on me even though she is much too big for that now. Muru is not a lap cat, but she will brush against my legs and curl on me as I drink my morning coffee and read.

Both of them still sleep a lot, for which I am grateful. That is also why most of these pictures are of them sleeping. The media in which they are awake are videos, which I have tons.

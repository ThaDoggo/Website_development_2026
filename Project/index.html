Why Do I Have Pets?

It feels good to look at cute, furry creatures, doesn't it? I certainly am a big fan, as are many likeminded people on the Internet. This page is dedicated to my pets - Kamu the teenage dog and Muru the cat. They are my fluffy friends and assistants who make sure I stick to a schedule and go out periodically because that is apparently good for me. They also fill my phone's gallery with videos and pictures of them doing adorable and weird pet things.
Both Kamu and Muru are very lovesome, but they have also caused me a fair bit of trouble, worry, work, cost, and things of that kind. But hey - they are really, really cute, right? Click the relevant navigations to see more of them and see what sort of high jinks they've gotten into.
